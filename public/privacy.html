Privacy Policy — Orbit Buddy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Orbit Buddy ("the app") is a personal AI companion. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Collect

  • Account information: email address and display name you provide at registration.
  • Conversations: messages you send to Orbit Buddy and its replies, stored so it can remember context across sessions.
  • Memories, goals, tasks, and routines: information you ask the app to remember or track.
  • Calendar feeds: if you add iCal feed URLs, the app periodically fetches public event data from those URLs.
  • Gmail (optional): if you connect your Google account, the app can send email on your behalf and search or read your inbox, only when you ask it to. Gmail data is never used for advertising and is never shared with third parties.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the app's core features: conversational AI, reminders, briefings, and connected-service actions you request.
  • Gmail access is used solely to perform the email actions you explicitly request (sending, searching, reading). The app does not scan your inbox in the background.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except as required to operate the service (hosting provider, AI model provider for generating replies).

Data Storage and Security

  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt; they are never stored in plain text.
  • OAuth tokens and calendar feed URLs are encrypted at rest.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS. Authentication uses secure, HTTP-only cookies with CSRF protection.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • You can delete individual memories, conversations, goals, and tasks at any time from within the app.
  • Disconnecting Gmail in the Connections tab immediately deletes your stored Gmail tokens.
  • To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact the developer (see below).

Google API Disclosure

Orbit Buddy's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • The app requests the gmail.send and gmail.readonly scopes.
  • Gmail data is used only to provide user-facing email features you request.
  • Gmail data is never used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI models.
  • Gmail data is never transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide the requested feature (sending via Google's own API).

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the developer through the app's Safety tab or at the support address listed on the homepage.
