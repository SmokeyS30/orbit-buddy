What Orbit Buddy does

Orbit Buddy is a privacy-first, self-hostable AI assistant designed to be a genuine daily companion — not just a chatbot. It learns your preferences over time, sends smart check-ins when they'd actually help, and connects to the services you already use.

💬 Natural conversations

Chat with an AI that remembers your goals, preferences, and past conversations — and gets smarter about you over time.

📧 Gmail integration

Connect your Gmail to send emails, search your inbox, and read messages — all by just asking. You always confirm before anything is sent.

📅 Calendar awareness

Add your iCal feeds and Orbit Buddy weaves your schedule into morning briefings and daily planning.

🎯 Goals & routines

Set goals, build routines, and get gentle nudges when you're falling behind — with specific suggestions to catch up.

🌤️ Daily briefings

Wake up to a briefing with your weather, today's calendar, and goal momentum — written like a friend, not a notification.

🔒 Privacy first

Your data is encrypted at rest. OAuth tokens are never stored in plain text. Disconnect any service at any time and its data is deleted immediately.

How Gmail integration works

When you connect Gmail, Orbit Buddy requests permission to send email on your behalf and read your inbox. This lets you say things like "email John about tomorrow's meeting" or "find that receipt from last week."

Important: Orbit Buddy always shows you the exact email — recipient, subject, and body — and waits for your explicit confirmation before sending anything. It never scans your inbox in the background, never uses your email for advertising, and never shares your data with third parties. See our privacy policy for the full details, including our compliance with Google's API Services User Data Policy.
