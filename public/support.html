How can we help?

Find quick answers, report a problem, or learn how to manage your Orbit account.

Account and sign-in help

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen to request a secure reset link. If you can sign in, use Safety → Report a Problem for private account help. Never include a password or recovery code.

Subscriptions

Orbit subscriptions are billed through Apple. You can restore a previous purchase from Orbit's subscription screen or manage and cancel billing in your Apple subscription settings.

Notifications

In Orbit, open Safety and choose Enable on this device. Also make sure notifications are allowed for Orbit Buddy in iPhone Settings.

Privacy and account deletion

You can disconnect optional integrations at any time. To permanently delete your Orbit account and associated data, open Safety → Delete account in the app.

Contact support

For general technical help, open a public Orbit Buddy support issue. Public issues are visible to others: do not include your account email, private conversations, passwords, recovery codes, API keys, or other secrets. Signed-in users can send a private report from Safety.
